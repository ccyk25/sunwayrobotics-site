Events we've run

Industry visits, competitions, talks and hands-on workshops. A look at some of what Sunway Robotics Club has been up to.

industry visit

Proton manufacturing plant visit

We swapped the lab for the factory floor. Hard hats on, the club walked through a Proton manufacturing plant to see what automation looks like when it is building cars at production scale. Same ideas our workshops teach, just a whole lot bigger.

competition

Robocon Malaysia 2025

The competition this club was started for. Our team brought robots built from scratch to that year's rulebook to the national round at UTHM, and tested them under the pressure of a live match clock. Everything we learned there feeds the road to ABU Robocon 2027.

industry visit

Festo industry visit

A visit to Festo, one of the big names in industrial automation. Up close at their Knowledge Bench we saw the pneumatic cylinders, valves and sensors that move real production lines, and how each part fits into a system that actually runs.

faculty showcase

ECX Week 2026

Five days of Engineering & Computing Xploration, the Faculty of Engineering and Technology's showcase week, with SRC as one of the clubs in the spotlight. We ran a booth of live demos, including a desk companion robot, an LED Tetris console and a joystick-driven robot arm, alongside our Servo Arm and Ottobot workshops.

competition

Mercedes-Benz Vibathon 2026

Our team AMGineers took on Mercedes-Benz Tech Malaysia's 2026 Vibathon and walked off the stage as runner-up. Proof that what members build in the lab holds up in front of industry judges.

hosted competition

Dodgebots 2025

Our own robot tournament. Teams started at a build workshop, spent two weeks building with mentors, then sent their robots into knockout matches on the arena floor until one was left standing. 12 teams, 36 students.

talk

Inside the Arena: combat robotics with Fikri

What does it take to build a robot that is meant to survive a fight? Fikri took a full lecture hall into the world of combat robotics: how the machines are designed, built and pushed to their limits in the arena.

workshop

Servo Arm Workshop

Build a MeArm robot arm from a flat kit, wire up its servos and drive it with a joystick on your phone over Bluetooth. No experience needed: by the end, the arm moves when your thumb does, and you understand the code that makes it happen.

workshop

Ottobot Workshop

Put together Otto, a small 3D-printed walking robot, then program it to take its first steps and react to what its ultrasonic “eyes” see. A friendly first build for anyone curious about robotics.

workshop

LED Tetris Workshop

Wire a grid of LED matrix modules and code a fully playable Tetris console from scratch. A crash course in electronics, power and game logic that ends with you playing the thing you just built.

workshop

PLC Workshop

Hands-on time with the programmable logic controllers that run real factories. On industrial PLC training rigs, members wrote the logic that switches, senses and sequences, just like on a production line.

workshop

TurtleBot Workshop

Build a small wheeled robot with an ultrasonic sensor and an LED matrix face, then code it to drive, sense and react to the world around it. Your first taste of a robot that makes its own decisions.

workshop

3D Modelling Workshop

A full room with one goal: turn an idea into a real part. Members learned to model in CAD, taking a rough first sketch all the way to a clean file ready for the 3D printer.

What we do

Six ways in
  1. 01

    Workshops

    Build and code a working robot in one session, from a phone-controlled arm to a walking bot to an industrial PLC. Members get in free or at half price.

    servo arm · ottobot · led tetris · plc · turtlebot · 3d modelling
  2. 02

    Projects

    Members design their own machines and show them at campus booths: a desk companion robot, an LED Tetris console, a photobooth that prints on the spot.

    desk companion · led tetris · photobooth
  3. 03

    Competitions we host

    Dodgebots is our own tournament: a build workshop, two weeks of building with mentors, then knockout matches on the arena floor.

    dodgebots 2025 · 12 teams · 36 students
  4. 04

    Competitions we enter

    Teams for Robocon and combat robotics, building to a rulebook and a deadline. Our AMGineers team took runner-up at the 2026 Mercedes-Benz Vibathon.

    robocon malaysia 2025 · abu robocon 2027 · combat robotics · vibathon
  5. 05

    Talks & visits

    Builders and engineers share what they've learned, and we visit the places where automation runs for real. Most recently: Inside the Arena with Fikri, and visits to Festo and Proton.

    inside the arena · festo · proton
  6. 06

    The Circuit

    Our weekly members' newsletter: what got built, what's coming up and what's worth applying for.

    weekly · for members
