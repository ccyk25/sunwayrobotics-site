Sponsorship tiers

Three ways to take part

Example tiers from Dodgebots 2025. Each event has its own proposal, with tiers set to its budget.

PlatinumHighest visibility
RM2,500
  • Dedicated feature post and three story mentions on Instagram and LinkedIn
  • Standalone bunting, plus the largest top-placed logo on poster, banner, bunting and certificates
  • Dedicated sponsor slide and top banner placement at the arena
  • Emcee mentions at opening, award and closing ceremonies on both days
  • Standalone thank-you post and feature in the highlight video
  • Framed certificate and 3D-printed award presented on stage
  • Full event photo package
Gold
RM1,800
  • One post and two story mentions before the event
  • Top-row logo on poster, banner, bunting and certificates
  • Side display near the audience area
  • Emcee mentions at the ceremonies
  • Shared thank-you post and logo in the highlight video
  • Framed certificate and 3D-printed award, delivered after the event
  • Included in the photo highlight set
Silver
RM1,000
  • Inclusion in the collective sponsor appreciation post
  • Logo on the bottom row of the bunting
  • Logo in the shared sponsor reel on screen
  • Emcee mention on competition day
  • Framed certificate and thank-you email

How it works

From first email to event day

  1. 01

    Request a proposal

    Tell us which event you'd like to support and we'll send its proposal and budget.

  2. 02

    Pick a monetary tier or an in-kind package.

  3. 03

    Sign the commitment form

    Complete the Partnership Commitment Form included with the proposal.
